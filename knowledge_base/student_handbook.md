# Student Handbook (sample knowledge document)

## Library hours
The campus library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. On Saturdays it is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Borrowing books
Students may borrow up to four books for 14 days. Books can be renewed once if nobody else has requested them. A late return fine of 5 rupees per book per day applies.

## Wi-Fi access
Connect to the network named Campus-Student. Sign in using your student ID and the password provided by the IT help desk. For password issues, visit the IT help desk in the computing lab during office hours.

## Exam registration
Exam registration is completed through the student portal. Students should register at least two weeks before the first exam. Contact the academic office if a course is missing from the portal.
